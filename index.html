The TLG Process

◎

Discover

We start by understanding your brand, what you want to say, and who you want to reach. This is where we listen, ask questions, and find the story worth telling.

→
◈

Once we know the story, we shape the idea around it - from the visual direction and mood to the way it should feel on screen.

◐
